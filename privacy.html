WOLF Transportation Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

WOLF Transportation ("we", "us") provides luxury ground transportation booking. This policy describes what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Booking information: pickup and drop-off locations, vehicle selection, and ride schedule, used to provide the service.
  • Contact information: details you provide when reserving or contacting us, used to confirm and coordinate rides.

How we use it

We use this information only to operate the service: reservations, dispatch, and support. We do not sell personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising.

Retention & deletion

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.
